How context-aware works here

A plain scanner flags every pattern equally. AI-Sentinel tracks whether user input (request.form, request.args, input) actually flows into the dangerous call, checks if the code sits on a Flask route, whether that route has a login check, and whether it touches passwords, payments or tokens. The risk score goes up for reachable, public, sensitive flaws, and down for unreachable or internal ones. Results are sorted by risk score, not just by bug type.

Standalone build of AI-Sentinel. A clean scan means none of the checked patterns matched — it is not a guarantee of perfect security.
